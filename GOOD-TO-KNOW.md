# Good to know

| Danish | English |
|---|---|
| husleje | rent |
| aconto (a/c varme, a/c vand) | utilities paid on account (heating, water), settled once a year |
| depositum | deposit (usually 3 months' rent, returned when you move out) |

| indflytningspris | move-in price: deposit + prepaid rent (+ sometimes the first month) |
| ledig fra / overtagelse | available from |
| snarest | as soon as possible |
| ubegrænset / tidsubegrænset | unlimited lease (no end date) |
| tidsbegrænset | fixed-term lease (ends on a set date) |
| 2 værelser (2 vær.) | 2 rooms = 1 bedroom + living room |
| lejlighed / rækkehus / hus | apartment / townhouse / house |
| værelse / delebolig | room / shared home |
| st. / kl. | ground floor / basement |
| 2. tv. / 2. th. / 2. mf. | 2nd floor, door on the left / right / middle |
| fremvisning / åbent hus | viewing / open house |
| venteliste | waiting list |
| husdyr tilladt | pets allowed |
| almen bolig | non-profit housing (see below) |
| andelsbolig | co-op home (you buy a share – not a rental) |

## Which accounts can be closed?

| Account | Risk | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **GitHub** | GitHub's terms say its servers (Actions) are for building and testing software. A watcher running around the clock is outside that. GitHub can stop the runs, switch off the repository or, in some cases, suspend the account. How likely that is, nobody knows. | Use an account only for Boligvagt (SETUP.md, step 7). Then the worst case is that Boligvagt stops, and you notice because the Monday message doesn't come. |
| **Your accounts on Boligportal, Findbolig and the other sites** | None from Boligvagt. It reads the sites without logging in, from GitHub's servers. If a site blocks it, the site blocks GitHub's servers, not you. | Use your normal accounts to write to landlords. Never give your login for a housing site to Boligvagt or to any other tool. |
| **Boligportal, if a tool writes for you** | Boligvagt deliberately doesn't write to landlords on Boligportal. It would need your login, Boligportal doesn't allow automated use, and your paid account could be closed. | Write to landlords on Boligportal yourself. Don't use apps or browser extensions that do it for you. |
| **CEJ and Kereby (automatic contact)** | No account is involved. But the message goes in your real name, and a landlord who notices automatic messages may ignore you. | Start with "test" and write a personal message (SETUP.md → *Automatic contact*). A separate email doesn't help, because the landlord must be able to reach you. |

## What does it cost to contact the landlord?

| Website | Cost to write to the landlord |
|---|---|
| Boligportal | Needs a subscription. 29 kr for the first 24 hours (new users only), then automatically 399 kr per 28 days. Also 499 kr for 2 months. Remember to cancel. |
| Findbolig | Free profile. Some homes require a waiting list, which can cost a fee. |
| CEJ, Kereby, Lejeboligmægleren, Norhjem, Capital Bolig, C.W. Obel, Taurus, Juli Living, City Apartment | Free. You write directly to the landlord on their own website. |

Boligportal's prices are from their help page (updated 8 Oct 2026). Check the
price before you buy; it changes. That the other sites are free is based on
their contact forms not asking for payment.

## Sign up for non-profit housing ("almen bolig")

Non-profit housing is the cheapest kind of rental, but it is handed out through
waiting lists, so Boligvagt can't help there. It can take years, so it's a good
idea to sign up now while you search.

The largest in Copenhagen:

- **KAB** – kab-bolig.dk
- **AAB** – aab.dk (sign-up 200 kr once, 2025 price)
- **fsb** – fsb.dk
- **3B** – 3b.dk
- **Lejerbo** – lejerbo.dk
- **Findbolig** – findbolig.nu (many housing organisations in one place)

Most charge a sign-up fee and a yearly fee to keep you on the list. By law the
fee may not exceed their cost of running the list. Remember to pay the yearly
fee, or you are removed from the list.
