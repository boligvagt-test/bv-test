# Message to the landlord

Landlords get many messages in a short time. The ones that are quick, short and
concrete get read. Write your message once now, so you can send it in seconds.

Writing in English is normal in Copenhagen. Don't use a machine translation into
Danish – the landlord will expect to speak Danish with you at the viewing.

Fill in the fields in `< >`, and write the last line about the specific home every time.

---

Hi,

I would love to see the home at **<address>** and can move in **<date / right away>**.

My name is <name>, I am <age> and I <live alone / will move in with …>.
I work as a <job title> <full time / part time> and earn <amount> DKK a month
before tax. I can show payslips.
I <don't smoke> and have <no pets / a cat>.

I can come to a viewing at short notice, also today or tomorrow.

<One line about this particular home, e.g. "I work nearby, and the balcony is exactly what I'm looking for.">

Best regards
<name>
Phone <phone>

---

**Tip:** Save the message in the Notes app so you can copy it in a moment. Also
save a short version as a text replacement: **Settings** → **General** →
**Keyboard** → **Text Replacement** → **+**. Then you type e.g. `home1` and the
whole text appears.

The automatic contact for CEJ and Kereby uses the same message. It is stored in
the secret `APPLICANT` (see **SETUP.md**), where `{address}` and
`{available_from}` are filled in automatically.
