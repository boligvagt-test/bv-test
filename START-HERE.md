# Boligvagt – start here

Boligvagt ("housing watch") checks 11 Danish rental websites around the clock and
notifies your phone within a few minutes when a home that fits you appears.

Someone runs Boligvagt for you, so all you need is the **Pushover** app on your
iPhone. It takes about 10 minutes.

## Set up your phone

1. Install **Pushover** from the App Store and open it. Tap **Create an account**,
   sign up with your email and confirm the email.
2. When the app asks to send notifications, tap **Allow**.
3. In the app, open **Settings**. Under **Alert Settings**:
   - Turn **on** **Critical Alerts for emergency-priority**. If the iPhone asks
     for permission, tap **Allow**. This makes your most important homes ring
     even when the phone is on silent.
   - Leave **Critical Alerts for high-priority** **off**. Otherwise every home
     breaks through silent mode, also at night.
4. At the top of **Settings** you see your **Pushover User Key**. Copy it and send
   it to the person who runs Boligvagt for you. The key only lets them send
   notifications to you.

## What happens next

- You get **Boligvagt has started**, and a test that rings every minute. Open it
  in Pushover and tap **Acknowledge Notification** to stop it.
- When a new home fits you, you get a notification with the price, size, address,
  move-in date and a link to the listing. Homes that match your most important
  criteria **ring every minute, even on silent**, until you tap **Acknowledge
  Notification**. Other homes give a normal notification.
- Every Monday at 9:00 you get a silent message saying Boligvagt is still running.
- If a website stops working, you get a warning. The person who runs Boligvagt
  gets it too, so you don't need to do anything. Use that site's own app
  meanwhile.

## Good to know

- **Be fast.** Cheap homes go within hours. Write your message to landlords now:
  see [MESSAGE-TEMPLATE.md](MESSAGE-TEMPLATE.md).
- **Danish words** in listings, what it costs to contact landlords, and how to
  sign up for non-profit housing: see [GOOD-TO-KNOW.md](GOOD-TO-KNOW.md).
- **To change your search** (price, area, size, rooms): tell the person who runs
  Boligvagt for you.
- **Tell them** if the Monday message doesn't come, or if the phone doesn't ring
  on silent.
- **Pushover is free for 30 days**, then $4.99 once. Buy it in the app: **Settings**
  → **Trial period**.
- Boligvagt never needs your passwords for any housing website. Don't give them
  to anyone.
